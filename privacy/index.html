Privacy Policy

Hummli is a private, offline-first loyalty-card wallet. The developer does not operate an account system, advertising network, analytics service or server that receives your card data.

Who is responsible

Hummli is developed and provided by Costantino Sertorio.

Information stored on your device

Hummli stores the information you choose to add—including shop names, barcode values and formats, notes, colours, logos, favourites, usage counts and optional card photos—in the app’s private storage on your iPhone. This information is used only to provide Hummli’s card storage, search, sorting and barcode display.

Hummli does not upload this information to its developer.

Camera and Photos

Hummli accesses the camera only when you choose to scan a barcode or take a card or logo photo. Images selected through Apple’s photo picker or captured with the camera are processed on the device for barcode recognition, cropping and storage. Hummli does not request access to your entire photo library. Saved card images are re-encoded to remove embedded metadata such as location information.

Website icon requests

Hummli does not choose a logo from a shop name. Only when you enter a website and select Use Website Icon, Hummli contacts that website to retrieve its published icons and may use Google’s public favicon service as a fallback. The requested domain and ordinary network information, including your IP address, may be received by those services under their own privacy practices. The developer does not receive these requests. The chosen icon is cached on your device.

iCloud

Private iCloud synchronisation is not enabled in the current version. If it is introduced, this policy will be updated before release. The intended design uses your private Apple iCloud storage rather than a developer-operated database.

Tracking, advertising and analytics

Hummli contains no advertising, analytics or cross-app tracking technology. It does not create a user account, build a shopping profile, request location or sell personal information.

Retention and deletion

Card information remains on your device until you delete the card or the app. You can delete an individual card from its Edit screen. Deleting Hummli removes its remaining local app data. Copies in device backups are controlled by your Apple backup settings and Apple’s applicable retention periods.

Security

Card data is kept inside Hummli’s iOS app container and benefits from the security and data-protection features of your device. No software can promise absolute security. Hummli reduces exposure by avoiding developer-operated card storage, accounts, advertising, analytics and location collection.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if Hummli’s features or data practices change. For a privacy question, use the support page. Never include a card number, barcode, card image or other sensitive information in a public support request.
