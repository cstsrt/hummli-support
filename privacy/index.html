Privacy Policy

Hummli is a private, offline-first loyalty-card wallet. There is no Hummli account, advertising network, analytics service or Hummli-operated server that receives your card data.

Information stored on your device

Hummli stores the information you choose to add—including shop names, barcode values and formats, notes, colours, logos, favourites, usage counts and optional card photos—in the app’s private storage on your iPhone. This information is used only to provide Hummli’s card storage, search, sorting and barcode display.

Hummli does not send your card data to us, advertisers or analytics services. When iCloud Drive is available, it synchronises through your private Apple iCloud database.

Camera and Photos

Hummli accesses the camera only when you choose to scan a barcode or take a card or logo photo. Images selected through Apple’s photo picker or captured with the camera are processed on the device for barcode recognition, cropping and storage. Hummli does not request access to your entire photo library. Saved card images are re-encoded to remove embedded metadata such as location information.

Website icon requests

Hummli does not choose a logo from a shop name. Only when you enter a website and select Use Website Icon, Hummli contacts that website to retrieve its published icons and may use Google’s public favicon service as a fallback. The requested domain and ordinary network information, including your IP address, may be received by those services under their own privacy practices. No request or browsing data is sent to us. The chosen icon is cached on your device.

iCloud

When iCloud Drive is available, Hummli automatically synchronises your cards through your private Apple iCloud account. Cards remain available locally for offline use. Your private CloudKit data is not accessible to us, and Hummli does not operate its own card database.

Tracking, advertising and analytics

Hummli contains no advertising, analytics or cross-app tracking technology. It does not create a user account, build a shopping profile, request location or sell personal information.

Retention and deletion

You can delete an individual card from its Edit screen; the deletion synchronises through iCloud when available. Deleting Hummli removes its local data, while cards already synchronised to your private iCloud database may remain available for recovery after reinstalling the app. Device backups and iCloud data are controlled by your Apple settings and Apple’s applicable retention periods.

Security

Card data is kept inside Hummli’s private iOS storage and, when available, your private Apple iCloud database. It benefits from Apple’s security and data-protection features. No software can promise absolute security. Hummli reduces exposure by operating no card server or account system and including no advertising, analytics or location collection.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if Hummli’s features or data practices change. For a privacy question, use the support page. Never include a card number, barcode, card image or other sensitive information in a public support request.
