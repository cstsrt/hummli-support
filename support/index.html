How can we help?

If Hummli is not behaving as expected, report the problem through GitHub. You do not need a Hummli account.

Open a support request
Keep card data private. Support requests are public. Never post a card number, barcode, card photo, receipt or other personal information.

Quick answers

Where are my cards stored?

In Hummli’s private storage on your iPhone. The current version does not use a Hummli account or developer-operated cloud database.

How do I import cards from another app?

Take a screenshot where the entire barcode is sharp and visible. In Hummli, add a card and choose Read from Screenshot.

How do I delete a card?

Open the card, choose Edit, then use the red Delete Card button at the bottom.

Why might a barcode not scan at the checkout?

Check that the saved number and detected format match the original card. Try the full-screen view and increase screen brightness. If the format cannot be generated reliably, attach a clear cropped photo of the original barcode.

How do I rearrange cards?

In Settings, choose Manual card order. Then hold and drag cards in the library. Favourites stay in their own section.
